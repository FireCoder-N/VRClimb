✅Connect the 2 tracking devices (placed on the 2 tripods diagonally in the room) to the power outlet.

> The two tracking devices should not change position or orientation under any
circumstances. For this reason, special black markings have been placed on the floor,
marking the position of the tripods.

✅Open the computer as well as the SteamVR application.

✅Make sure that 2 USB adaptors are correctly connected to the front USB ports of the computer.

Both the adaptors and the ports are marked with appropriate identifiers, `A`, `B`.

✅Activate the four motion sensors in order, according to the number (i-iv) that
is written on them.

> Press and hold the blue button (triangular shaped) on the top of each device. After about 3 seconds, the light will turn solid green or blue. Green indicates normal operation of the device, while blue indicates that it is in coordination mode with the rest of the system.
> <br> In the event that the light remains blue, make sure that you have followed the above connectivity steps correctly.
> <br> By pressing and holding the same button while the sensor is on, it will first enter pairing mode (where the LED color is flashing blue) and then turn off, with the light remaining off.

✅Wear the sensors on the corresponding parts of your body, with the light always facing forward.

> For the upper limb sensors, first insert your thumb into the special opening and tighten
the strap around your wrist, making sure that the sensor is located
in the center of the back of the palm (metacarpus) as much as possible.

✅Put on the VR mask and make sure that you see all the trackers in the SteamVR environment.

✅Open the `MyProject.uproject` file or its shortcut on the desktop.

✅Press the green `Play` button in the center of the top of the screen and enjoy the climbing experience in a VR environment!

✅To stop the application, press the `esc` button on the keyboard.
